How check-in works

  1. Find your reservation by flight number, booking code or personal document.
  2. Pick the flight you want to check in for.
  3. Confirm — you get your boarding gate and boarding time.

Check-in opens 72 hours before departure and closes at departure. Flights whose check-in is not open yet are listed, but cannot be confirmed.
